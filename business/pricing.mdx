## Monetization Strategy

CreatorFlow operates on a Freemium + Subscription model, offering tiered pricing based on team size, storage needs, and advanced feature access.

### Pricing Tiers
| Tier | Price | Target Users | Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | $0 | Individual creators, small teams (1-3) | 5GB storage, 1 team, basic collaboration. |
| **Pro** | $19/mo per team | Professional teams (3-10) | 100GB storage, unlimited projects, advanced tools. |
| **Business** | $49/mo per team | Agencies, production houses (10-25)| 500GB storage, priority support, custom roles, analytics. |
| **Enterprise**| Custom | Large organizations | Unlimited storage, SSO, SLA, dedicated support. |

### Additional Revenue Streams
- **Storage Add-ons**: Purchase an additional 50GB for $5/mo.
- **AI Credits**: Pay-per-use for advanced AI features like auto-captioning and content scoring.
- **Professional Services**: Onboarding, training, and custom workflow integrations.
- **White-label Solutions**: Custom branding for production and marketing agencies.

### Why Freemium?
We believe in supporting creators at every stage of their journey. Our free tier allows individuals to experience the core power of CreatorFlow, while our paid tiers provide the scale and advanced tools required for professional teams to thrive.
