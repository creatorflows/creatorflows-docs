## Growth & Development Roadmap

We follow a structured, phased approach to platform development, ensuring stability while rapidly delivering value to our users.

### Phase 1: MVP (Months 1-3)
Focus on core team and workspace functionality.
- Workspace creation and management.
- File upload/download with version control.
- Basic text commenting and role-based access control.
- Initial calendar integration.

### Phase 2: Collaboration (Months 4-6)
Enhancing the team communication experience.
- Voice comments and annotations.
- AI auto-captions for video assets.
- @mentions and notification center.
- Advanced search functionality.

### Phase 3: Monetization (Months 7-9)
Scaling the platform for professional teams.
- Launch of Pro and Business subscription plans.
- Integrated payment processing.
- Additional storage add-on options.
- Detailed team usage analytics.

### Phase 4: AI Integration (Months 10-12)
Leveraging AI to optimize creative workflows.
- Grammar check for scriptwriters.
- AI-assisted rough cuts and video editing assistance.
- Content performance prediction models.
- Smart tagging and categorization.

### Future Roadmap
- Direct social media publishing.
- Client-only review portals.
- Advanced video processing pipelines (FFmpeg).
- Integration with major cloud storage providers (S3/R2).
