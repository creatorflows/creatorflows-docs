# Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about using our platform.

## Account & Billing

??? faq "How do I create an account?"
    You can sign up in three ways:
    
    1. **Email registration**: Visit [web.fanaan.bilsul.com](https://web.creatorflows.com)
    2. **Social login**: Use Google, GitHub, or Discord for instant setup
    3. **Team invitation**: Click the link sent by your team admin
    
    [Learn more about account creation →](../getting-started/create-account.md)

??? faq "Is there a free tier?"
    Yes! Our free tier includes:
    - 500 tokens per month
    - 10 project limit
    - 1GB file storage
    - 3 export formats (glTF, OBJ, STL)
    - Community support via Telegram
    
    [Compare all plans →](../pricing/plans.md)

??? faq "How do I cancel my subscription?"
    1. Go to **Account Settings** → **Billing**
    2. Click **"Manage Subscription"**
    3. Select **"Cancel Plan"**
    4. Confirm your choice
    
    Your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period.

## Projects & Assets

??? faq "How many projects can I create?"
    | Plan | Project Limit | Storage |
    |------|---------------|---------|
    | Free | 10 projects | 1GB |
    | Pro | Unlimited | 10GB |
    | Team | Unlimited | 100GB |
    
    Archived projects don't count toward your limit.

??? faq "How long are my assets stored?"
    - **Active projects**: Stored indefinitely
    - **Free tier**: 90 days of history
    - **Paid tiers**: Unlimited storage
    - **Deleted projects**: 30-day recovery period

## Privacy & Security

??? faq "Is my data private?"
    Absolutely. We take privacy seriously:
    
    - Your projects are private by default
    - We don't share your data with third parties
    - Enterprise plans include data residency options
    - GDPR compliant
    
    [Read our privacy policy →](../legal/privacy.md)

??? faq "How do I enable two-factor authentication?"
    1. Go to **Account Settings** → **Security**
    2. Click **"Enable 2FA"**
    3. Scan QR code with Google Authenticator
    4. Enter the 6-digit code
    5. Save backup codes in a secure place
