## AI-Powered Auto-Captions

CreatorFlow includes advanced AI integration to automatically generate captions for your video assets, making them more accessible and social-media-ready.

### Generating Captions
- **Automated Processing**: Uploaded videos automatically have auto-captions generated within 24 hours.
- **Accuracy**: Our AI model provides high-accuracy transcription for English content, with additional language support coming soon.

### Managing Captions
- **Edit & Correct**: Users can easily edit any auto-generated captions to ensure 100% accuracy.
- **On/Off Toggle**: Captions can be toggled on and off directly within the video player.
- **Format Support**: Download caption files in industry-standard formats like SRT and VTT.

### Benefits
- **Accessibility**: Make your videos accessible to all audiences, including those with hearing impairments.
- **Social Media Ready**: Optimize your content for social platforms where many users watch videos with the sound off.
- **Time-Saving**: Eliminate the manual effort of transcribing videos yourself.

### Future Roadmap
- **Multilingual Support**: Support for additional languages and translations.
- **Manual Upload**: Option to upload your own SRT/VTT files for existing video assets.
- **Smart Tagging**: Use AI to automatically tag video content based on the generated captions.
