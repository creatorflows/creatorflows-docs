## Visual Feedback with Annotations

CreatorFlow includes a powerful annotation system that allows team members to draw and mark directly on images and video frames.

### Drawing Tools
- **Shapes & Markers**: Use arrows, rectangles, and freehand drawing to highlight specific areas of an asset.
- **Layering**: Annotations are saved as overlays, preserving the original asset.
- **Color Coding**: Different team members can use unique colors for their annotations.

### Annotation Management
- **Linked Comments**: Each annotation can be linked to a specific text or voice comment for more context.
- **Video Overlays**: Mark specific frames in a video to give precise feedback on visual elements.
- **Visibility Control**: Toggle annotations on and off as needed while reviewing an asset.

### Why Annotations?
- **Clearer Communication**: Eliminate guesswork by visually showing exactly what needs to be changed.
- **Faster Revisions**: Editors and Designers can quickly see and address specific visual feedback.
- **Collaborative Review**: Multiple team members can contribute their own annotations simultaneously.

### Feature Details
- **Cross-Platform Compatibility**: View and manage annotations from both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Asset Integration**: Annotations are stored as metadata associated with specific file versions.
- **Exporting Reviews**: Include visual annotations in exported project review reports.
