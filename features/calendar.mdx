## Shared Team Calendar

CreatorFlow includes a built-in calendar for teams to organize production schedules, deadlines, and publishing dates.

### Managing Deadlines
- **Automatic Scheduling**: Tasks with due dates automatically appear on the team calendar.
- **Project Overviews**: View all project milestones and deadlines in one place.
- **Reminders**: File deadlines trigger notifications 24 hours before they are due.

### Creating Events
- **Custom Events**: Team Leads can create calendar events for meetings, reviews, or shoots.
- **Color Coding**: Categorize events by project or type for easy visual tracking.
- **Drag-and-Drop**: Reschedule events by simply dragging them to new dates.

### Calendar Views
Switch between multiple views to match your planning needs:
- **Month View**: Get a high-level overview of the production schedule.
- **Week/Day View**: Drill down into daily tasks and deadlines.
- **Agenda View**: See a chronological list of upcoming events.

### Integrations & Exporting
- **External Sync**: Export your CreatorFlow calendar to Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar (iCal).
- **Project Links**: Directly click on a calendar deadline to open the associated file or project.
- **Team Availability**: View when team members are available or working on specific tasks.

### Team Notifications
- **Stay Informed**: Get notified when new events are added or when deadlines are approaching.
- **Mobile Access**: View and manage your production schedule from any device.
