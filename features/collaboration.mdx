## Real-Time Collaboration

CreatorFlow makes it easy for teams to communicate directly on content assets, reducing the need for external tools like Slack or email.

### Text & Voice Comments
- **Text Comments**: Leave traditional text feedback on any file.
- **Voice Comments**: Record up to 2 minutes of audio directly in the browser. Perfect for detailed explanations.

### Timestamps & Video Feedback
- **Precise Timing**: Add comments to specific timestamps in videos (e.g., "Fix audio at 1:23").
- **Interactive Navigation**: Click any timestamp in the comment thread to jump to that exact frame in the video player.

### Team Communication
- **@Mentions**: Tag team members to notify them of specific comments or feedback.
- **Replies & Threads**: Keep conversations organized with nested replies.
- **Activity Feed**: View a real-time feed of all team interactions and file updates.

### Managing Feedback
- **Edit & Delete**: Original authors and Team Leads can edit or delete comments.
- **Attachments**: Attach screenshots or reference files directly to your comments.
- **Notifications**: Receive instant in-app, email, or push notifications for @mentions and updates.

### Activity Feed
Stay updated with a dedicated team activity feed:
- **Project Tracking**: See who uploaded what, and when.
- **Searchable history**: Easily find past feedback or file status changes.
- **Configurable preferences**: Tailor how you receive notifications to match your workflow.
