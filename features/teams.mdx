## Managing Your Teams

CreatorFlow allows Team Leads to create and manage multiple workspaces, each with its own set of members, roles, and content.

### Creating a Team
Team Leads can create unlimited teams/workspaces to separate different projects or client work.
- Each team has a unique URL/slug (e.g., `creatorflow.com/team/creative-co`).
- Team settings include team name, description, logo, and custom branding options.

### Inviting Members
Collaborate with others by inviting them to join your workspace.
- Team Leads send invites via email with a pre-assigned role.
- Invitations expire after 7 days for security.
- Team members can accept or decline invitations from their own dashboard.

### Managing Members
- Team Leads can view a list of all team members, their roles, and when they joined.
- Roles can be updated at any time.
- Members can be removed from a team by the Team Lead.
- Individual members can leave teams voluntarily.

### Activity Logging
The platform tracks all major member actions for transparency and security, including:
- File uploads and downloads
- Commenting activity
- Status updates
- Permission changes

<Note>
  Activity logs are essential for auditing and understanding how your team is progressing on projects.
</Note>
