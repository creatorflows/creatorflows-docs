## Introduction

CreatorFlow is a comprehensive collaboration platform that enables seamless workflow management, file sharing, and real-time collaboration among diverse content creation roles including video editors, scriptwriters, graphic designers, social media managers, and on-camera talent.

### The Problem

Modern content creation teams often struggle with fragmented workflows, juggling multiple disconnected tools like Google Drive, Trello, WhatsApp, Slack, and WeTransfer. This leads to:
- Workflow inefficiencies
- Version control nightmares
- Communication gaps

### The Solution

CreatorFlow addresses these critical fragmentation problems by providing a unified environment for:
- **Team Workspaces**: Dedicated environments for content teams to organize their work.
- **Role-Based Access Control**: Granular permissions managed by team leads.
- **File Management System**: Upload, download, version control, and in-platform preview.
- **Collaboration Tools**: Text/voice comments, auto-captions, and annotations.
- **Calendar Integration**: Production schedules, deadlines, and content calendars.
- **Storage Management**: Centralized repository for all team assets.
