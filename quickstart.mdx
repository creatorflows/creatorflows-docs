## Setting up your Workspace

Follow these steps to set up your team's workspace and start collaborating.

### 1. Register & Login
Create your account using your email and password or use Google OAuth for a faster setup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Sign up with your professional email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email">
    Check your inbox for an OTP code to verify your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Login">
    Log in to access your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Create a Team
As a Team Lead, you can create a dedicated workspace for your content team.

- Go to the **Teams** section in the sidebar.
- Click **Create Team**.
- Choose a unique team name and URL slug (e.g., `creatorflow.com/team/creative-co`).

### 3. Invite Members
Invite your editors, designers, and scriptwriters to join the team.

- Navigate to **Team Settings**.
- Enter member email addresses and assign them specific **Roles**.
- Members will receive an email invitation to join your workspace.

### 4. Create your first Project
Organize your content into projects.

- Click **New Project** in your workspace.
- Set a project name, description, and optional due date.
- You're now ready to start uploading files!

<Tip>
  Check out our [User Roles & Access](/user-roles) documentation to learn more about permissions.
</Tip>
